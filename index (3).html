How to play (Classic)
  1. Click / touch anywhere on the game and drag to aim. Longer drag = more force (N).
  2. Release to jump. Land on the next building to score.
  3. Falling off, or flying above the red line, ends the run.
🎯 Mission: land exactly on the flag
Level roofs, no air drag, 20 px = 1 m. Every mission hides a different unknown: force, speed, angle, contact time, mass, range, flight time, height, kinetic energy or momentum. Solve it, enter it with units and launch. You must land within ±0.3 m of the flag; anything else overshoots or undershoots. Use at least 1 decimal place.
